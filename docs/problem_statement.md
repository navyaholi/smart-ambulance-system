# Problem Statement

## Smart Ambulance Route & Hospital Allocation System

During medical emergencies, an ambulance needs to reach the patient quickly and transport the patient to a suitable hospital.

The system must consider:

- Emergency priority
- Ambulance availability
- Ambulance location
- Patient location
- Hospital availability
- Available beds
- Emergency capacity
- Road distances

## Objective

The objective is to develop a Python-based system that uses computational thinking and algorithms to assist in ambulance allocation, route selection, and hospital selection.

## Problem Decomposition

The problem is divided into the following smaller problems:

1. Store patient information.
2. Store ambulance information.
3. Store hospital information.
4. Prioritize emergency cases.
5. Find the nearest available ambulance.
6. Find a suitable hospital.
7. Calculate the shortest route.
8. Optimize emergency resources.
9. Check hospital availability concurrently.
10. Test the complete system.

## Expected Outcome

The system should provide an organized algorithmic approach for handling emergency ambulance allocation and routing.
